The Foundation: Axilwood Timber

For generations, the timber trade in India has relied on opaque local networks and middlemen, leaving landowners unsure of the true value of their hard-grown assets. We built the Axilwood App to completely transparentize this market.

It operates as a direct marketplace. Landowners across the country can post photos and specific details of their trees—including girth, height, and truck accessibility. Buyers, ranging from independent cutters to large mill owners, use our location-based algorithms to discover timber nearby. With secure in-app chat and Pro ad-boosting capabilities, we eliminate the intermediaries, ensuring fair prices for sellers and a reliable supply chain for buyers.

The Evolution: Modern Life Events

Building a secure, highly scalable marketplace taught us how to engineer robust digital architecture. We took that exact same engineering DNA and applied it to a completely different sector: modern life events.

Axilwood Invitations was created to bring flawless design, interactive technology, and seamless logistics to wedding planning. We empower couples and event planners to craft breathtaking digital invitations in minutes, track live RSVPs globally, and share their stories without the friction of traditional paper logistics.
